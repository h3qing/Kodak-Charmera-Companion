Everything your keychain camera needs

📸

Auto-Import

Plug in your camera and photos are detected automatically. A popup appears with the photo count — one click to import everything.

🧠

Local AI Labeling

Each photo is analyzed by a vision model running entirely on your machine. No cloud uploads, no API keys, no subscriptions.

✏️

Smart Rename

Configure your naming pattern with tokens like {content} and {YYYY}. Files are renamed directly on the SD card.

🎞️

Vintage Effects

10 film effects (vintage, noir, grain, light leak...) and 4 frames (polaroid, film strip...) to enhance your photos.

🔍

Search & Browse

Full-text search across AI descriptions. Filter by tags. Multi-select and batch export to any folder.

🔒

100% Private

Everything runs locally — Rust backend, SQLite database, local AI model. Your photos never leave your machine.

Built with serious tools

Native performance. Desktop-first. No Electron.
